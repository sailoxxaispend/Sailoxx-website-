The Problem

Sound familiar?

Most companies adopting AI are flying blind. Here is what we see in almost every business we audit.

💸

No one owns the full picture

Marketing bought Jasper. IT bought Copilot. Ops built an automation. Nobody has a complete list of what you're actually paying for.

📊

Zero ROI visibility

Your CFO or board asks for ROI proof on AI spend. You have no baseline data, no usage metrics, no answer. This is embarrassing and dangerous.

🔁

Duplicate tools everywhere

Three different teams are paying for three tools that do the same thing. Nobody realised because nobody mapped the landscape.

👻

Ghost subscriptions

Tools bought with enthusiasm six months ago that nobody actually uses. The auto-renewal came and went. Money is leaving your account monthly for nothing.

⚠️

Outputs nobody trusts

Your team uses the AI tool but spends 40% of the time fixing its output. The real cost is not the subscription — it's the wasted hours of skilled people.

🔮

No internal objectivity

The person who championed a tool cannot honestly report it isn't working. You need an outside voice with no stake in the outcome.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you need access to our internal systems?
+
No. We never need login credentials or system access. We work from a tool inventory you fill in, software invoices you share, and an anonymous employee survey your team fills at their own time. All data handling is confidential and covered by an NDA before we start.
How much time does this take from our team?
+
Minimal. The manager filling the tool inventory sheet needs about 20–30 minutes. Individual team members filling the survey need 5 minutes each. The only calls required are one 20-minute kickoff and one 30-minute debrief. Total time from your team is under 2 hours across 5 days.
What if we only have 3–4 AI tools? Is it still worth it?
+
Sometimes the most valuable finding is that 3 tools are doing the job of 1. Even small tool stacks often have significant waste. We'll tell you on the discovery call if the math doesn't justify an audit for your specific situation.
Is the survey really anonymous for employees?
+
Yes. The survey has no name field required. We never share individual responses with your management team. We only present aggregated findings in the report. Employees can be honest about what isn't working without fear of attribution.
What if the audit finds we're already spending efficiently?
+
Then you have documented proof that your AI spend is justified and optimised — something you can show your board, CFO, or investors. In our experience though, every company we've audited has found at least one meaningful saving.
Can you help us implement the recommendations?
+
Yes. Our monthly monitoring retainer includes implementation follow-up — we track whether the changes in the roadmap are happening, flag if anything is drifting, and advise on any new tools your team wants to adopt.
