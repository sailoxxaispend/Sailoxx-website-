The problem

Most companies adopting AI cannot see the full picture.

Tools get bought team by team, renewals roll over, and nobody owns the total. These are the patterns an AI spend review is built to surface.

Read the full problem

  1. No one owns the full picture

    Marketing bought Jasper. IT bought Copilot. Ops built an automation. Nobody has a complete list of what you are actually paying for.

  2. No ROI visibility

    Your CFO or board asks for proof of return on AI spend. You have no baseline data, no usage metrics and no answer.

  3. Duplicate tools

    Three teams pay for three tools that do the same job. Nobody noticed because nobody mapped the landscape.

How it works

Five days to a clear answer.

Low friction for your team, high precision in the output. No lengthy consultancy engagement and no disruption to your workflow.

  1. Discovery call

    A focused 20-minute call to understand your company size, current AI tools and goals.

    20 minutes, Day 0

  2. Tool inventory

    You fill a simple spreadsheet with every AI tool, its cost and its department. We cross-check it against your invoices to catch anything missed.

    Google Sheet, Day 1

  3. Employee usage survey

    A structured 10-question survey for your team. It takes 5 minutes each and captures real usage, time saved and trust levels, anonymously.

    Async survey, Days 1 to 2

  4. ROI calculation

    We run every tool through our ROI model, comparing the value generated with the actual cost. Numbers come only from your own data.

    Data analysis, Day 3

  5. Report and debrief

    A plain-English report with a verdict for every tool, a savings roadmap with exact rupee amounts, and a 30-minute walkthrough call.

    Full report, Day 5
